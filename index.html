Privacy Policy

Alphabet Adventure · Effective date: October 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Alphabet Adventure (“we”, “our”, or “the app”) handles information when you use our mobile application. The app helps children learn English, Hindi, and Punjabi letters with sounds and pictures.

Summary

  • We do not require an account or login.
  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not use third-party advertising or analytics SDKs in the app.
  • Letter audio and images are included in the app and work without sending your data to us.

Information we collect

We do not collect personal information from you or your child through the app. We do not ask for names, email addresses, phone numbers, photos, or precise location.

The app may store a theme preference (for example, light or night mode) on your device using local storage in the app’s WebView. This stays on your device and is not transmitted to us.

Children’s privacy

Alphabet Adventure is designed for young learners. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us using the email below and we will take appropriate steps to delete it if we ever received it.

Internet permission

The Android app declares the INTERNET permission because the app framework may use network capabilities. In normal use, learning content (audio and images) is bundled in the app. The app is not designed to upload your usage or personal data to our servers.

Data sharing

We do not share, sell, or rent personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Data retention and deletion

Because we do not collect personal information on our servers, there is nothing for us to retain or delete on our side. You can remove all app data by uninstalling the app or clearing app storage in your device settings.

Security

We aim to protect information stored on your device through standard platform security. No method of storage is 100% secure.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the new version on this page and update the effective date above. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.
